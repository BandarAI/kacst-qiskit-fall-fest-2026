# Glossary: English → Arabic

**Status**
- **Quantabic**: the term is in the Quantabic glossary (quantabic.com), or Quantabic uses it consistently.
- **Proposed ✱**: we proposed the term. Check it against the SQA dictionary or Quantabic before the final pitch.

## Writing rules
- Gate letters, kets and symbols stay in Latin script: H, X, RZZ, |0⟩, γ, β.
- The first time a proposed term appears, give the English in brackets, e.g. «طبقة الخلط (mixer layer)».
- Use Western digits (0–9) in both languages, as on the site.
